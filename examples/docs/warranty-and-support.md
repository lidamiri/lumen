Warranty and Support

Every device carries a limited warranty of 24 months from the date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid exposure, or unauthorized repairs.

Support is available by email on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00 Central European Time. Priority support is included with the premium plan and responds within 4 hours.

Firmware updates are published monthly. Security patches for critical vulnerabilities are released as soon as they are verified, usually within 72 hours.
